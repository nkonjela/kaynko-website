How it works

Up and running in minutes.

1

Download & set up

Install from Google Play. Add your business name, products, and prices. Under 5 minutes, no help needed.

2

Open your session

Tap "Open Session", count your opening float, and you're live. Your cashier logs in with a PIN.

3

Sell and close

Complete a sale in under 10 seconds. At day's end, count your cash and read your report. Done.

What’s inside

Built for the shop floor.

Lightning Checkout

Complete a sale in under 10 seconds. Search products, apply discounts, take payment — done.

Works Offline

No internet? No problem. All data lives on the device. Sync when you're back online.

Real-Time Inventory

Stock levels update on every sale. Low-stock alerts so you never run out of your best sellers.

Team Management

Multiple cashiers, PIN login, manager oversight, and activity tracking per staff member.

Reports & Export

Daily summaries, sales history, and end-of-day reports. Export to PDF, Excel, or Google Sheets.

Connect to Other Apps

Automatically send your sales data to other business tools and software. Available on Growth plan and up.

Flexible Payments

Receipts & Quotations
