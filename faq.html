Getting Started

What is Kaynko POS?

A mobile-first point-of-sale app for small and growing retailers. You can create products, make sales, track stock and debt, and understand your business — all from your phone, with no training required.

Does Kaynko POS work without internet?

Yes. Kaynko is offline-first — sales, stock, receipts, and reports all work without a connection. Features that need the internet (like connected payments, Google Sheets sync, or cloud backup) simply wait until you're back online.

What devices does Kaynko POS run on?

Android phones and tablets today. It's built mobile-first rather than adapted from desktop software.

How much does it cost?

Starter is $5/month for one user, Growth is $12/month for up to five users, and Business is $20/month for unlimited users. Every plan starts with a free 14-day trial. See the full breakdown on the pricing section.

Is my business data safe?

Your data is stored in an encrypted database on your own device. You can also enable automatic backup to your own Google Drive from Settings, so a lost or broken phone doesn't mean losing your business records.

Can my staff use Kaynko too?

Yes — add cashiers with their own PIN under Growth or Business plans. You control what each cashier can do, and sensitive actions (like voids or returns) can require manager approval.

Payments

Do I need a Paynow account to use Kaynko POS?

No. Every payment method works without one — cash, mobile money, card, bank transfer, and credit all work from day one. You'll just record non-cash payments yourself instead of having them confirmed automatically. See how payments work for the full picture.

Is there a fee to connect Paynow?

Connecting is free. Paynow charges its own processing fees per transaction — check Paynow's own fee schedule for current rates, since Kaynko doesn't set or collect them.

Where does my money go when a customer pays by card or mobile money?

Directly to your own bank account — the one you registered with Paynow. Kaynko never touches, holds, or has access to your money. See Where does the money go? for more detail.

What happens if a customer's payment fails?

No sale is created. Nothing is charged, and you can ask the customer to try again or choose a different payment method.

Can I connect a different payment provider instead of Paynow?

Not yet. Paynow is the only connected provider today. Other providers are listed in Settings → Payments for visibility, but aren't connectable yet — we'd rather be upfront about that than show a button that doesn't work.

Support

How do I get help if something goes wrong?

Email support@kaynko.app, or check the in-app Help section under Settings for step-by-step guides.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, there's no contract or lock-in. Manage or cancel your subscription any time from your device's app store subscription settings.
