Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data Storage

Kaynko POS stores all business data (sales, products, customers, inventory) locally on your Android device in an encrypted SQLite database. Data does not leave your device unless you explicitly use a sync feature (Google Sheets) or configure webhooks.

2. Data We Collect

We collect crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability. These reports contain device information and app state at the time of the crash. No sales data or personal customer information is included in crash reports.

If you subscribe to a paid plan, your subscription is managed by RevenueCat. Their privacy policy applies to subscription data.

3. Analytics & Advertising

We use Firebase Analytics and Meta (Facebook) App Events to understand how people set up and use Kaynko, and to measure the results of our own advertising. These tools collect app-usage signals — for example, that setup was completed, a trial started, a first sale was recorded, or a subscription was purchased (and its price) — along with standard device information and your device's advertising identifier, used to measure and improve ad performance.

We never send these tools your product names, customer details, receipts, payment credentials, or inventory records. Nothing you sell or who you sell it to ever leaves your device through this path.

You can limit ad personalization for your device in your Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, which these tools respect.

4. Google Sheets Integration

If you connect your Google account, Kaynko creates a spreadsheet in your Google Drive. The app only accesses spreadsheets it creates — it cannot read or modify any other files in your Drive. You can disconnect at any time from Settings → Export & Sync.

5. Automatic Backup

If you enable backup, Kaynko uploads an encrypted copy of your business database to a private, app-only folder in your own Google Drive (not visible in your regular Drive file list, and not accessible to us). It is encrypted with a key derived from your own PIN before it ever leaves your device — we cannot read it. You can trigger a manual backup or restore, or turn automatic backup off, at any time from Settings.

6. Webhooks

If you configure webhooks, Kaynko sends sales and event data to the HTTPS endpoints you specify. You are responsible for the security of those endpoints.

7. Affiliate Program

If you apply to Kaynko's affiliate program, we collect the contact details and payout information (such as a bank account or mobile money number) you submit, in order to verify your application and pay commissions. This information is stored separately from any business sales data, is never shown in founder-review listings, and is only accessed at actual payout time.

8. Security

The local database is encrypted with AES-256. Sensitive credentials (encryption keys, API keys, webhook secrets) are stored in Android Keystore via FlutterSecureStorage.
